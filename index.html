How Glitch'n started

Glitch'n began in June 2025 with three free summer courses at the ASU MIX Center in downtown Mesa, Arizona. Sixty young people and their families came to build circuits that make sound. These lessons carry that course forward, so more people can build along.

Glitch'n is a project of the School of Games, Arts, Media and Engineering at Arizona State University.
